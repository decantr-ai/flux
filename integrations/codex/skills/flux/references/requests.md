# Reviewed questions, changes and answers

`requests --pending --session <id>` lists prepared or queued outgoing diagnostics. Check the exact request first. If completed, use the stored result rather than repeating it.

A Flux audit is read-only. Use only its supplied evidence and current direction, within its stated budget. Separate verified facts, reports and gaps; cite supplied source aliases. Do not fetch excluded history, revise plans, resume old work or dispatch another request. Write an answer of at most 200 words to a UTF-8 file, then use `result <request-id> --file <answer-file> --session <id>`. Prefer the exact callback printed by the packet. If unavailable, answer in chat and state that the dashboard result was not recorded.

A reviewed change request authorizes only its exact feedback and explicitly affected items. Other listed items are context. Inspect current files, preserve unrelated progress, and record a source-linked checkpoint when meaningful. The queued instruction record is the direction anchor; its generated envelope is not a new user request.

A `Flux answer submission <id>` contains user-reviewed answers paired with original question identities. Apply those answers to their questions and existing task; do not resend them or write a diagnostic result. Flux records receipt after validating the captured message against the frozen submission. Queue acceptance alone is not receipt. Native question controls may remain open; do not claim they were synchronized.
